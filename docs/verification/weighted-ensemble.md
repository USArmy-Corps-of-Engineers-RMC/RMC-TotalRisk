# Weighted Ensemble Verification

**Test class:** `WeightedEnsembleVerification` · **Tests:** 4 · **Run of record:** 2026-08-27, isolated run, ✅ all passed

The optional epistemic realization weights generalize every ensemble reduction: the mean of a
measure becomes Σw·x/Σw, the percentile bands and summary percentiles use the symmetric weighted
percentile (each positive-weight realization at plotting position p(i) = A(i)/(A(i) + B(i)), A
the weight strictly below and B strictly above, linear interpolation between positions), and the
convergence indicators report the weighted mean with standard error √(V/N_eff) — V the
reliability-weighted unbiased variance and N_eff = (Σw)²/Σw² the Kish effective sample size,
recorded as `EnsembleSummary.EffectiveRealizationCount`. Weights are results-side state
([results-catalog](../technical-reference/results-catalog.md)): never hashed, never an influence
on sampling seeds, absent from unweighted payloads. The library computes the weighted reductions
through the Numerics weighted statistics, so this family's oracle **re-implements every reduction
from its definition** — its own pair sort, prefix sums, plotting positions, bracket search, and
moment formulas — and never calls the library's weighted paths.

## Scenarios

**Synthetic fixed-seed ensembles.** Fifty-slot ensembles generated by `MersenneTwister(24680)`
(and offsets +1, +2 for the weights and the replication fixture): Total means uniform on (0, 10),
failure probabilities uniform on (0, 0.1), values-at-risk uniform on (0, 100) with NaN injected
at every seventh slot, weights uniform with exact zeros forced at every eleventh slot — the NaN
pairwise filter, the zero-weight exclusion, and the full four-slot reduction all exercised on one
fixture.

**Engine scenario.** The trivial uncertain single-component fixture — stage frequency
(0.999 → 0 ft, 0.5 → 10 ft, 0.001 → 30 ft), a triangular-ordinate uncertain fragility rising
from 10 ft to 20 ft, linear failure consequences (0 → 0, 30 → 300) with a non-failure mode
(30 → 60) — at N = 100 realizations under the deterministic index-varying weights
w(i) = 0.25 + ((37·i) mod 11), run weighted and unweighted from identical content.

Coverage note: the per-ordinate weighted band assembly and the scalar band assembly share the
identical upstream weighted-percentile call, and the interpolated per-realization curves the
per-ordinate path reduces are not retained after a run — so the independent scalar-reduction
oracle (check 3) plus the fast suite's unit-weight band identity carry the curve-band evidence.
